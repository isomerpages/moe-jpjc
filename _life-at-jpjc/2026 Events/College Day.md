<p>🎉 On 24 July 2026, we celebrated our&nbsp;<b>8th College Day</b>&nbsp;with the theme&nbsp;<b>"Celebrating Our Journey, Crossing New Frontiers."</b>&nbsp;The occasion was a meaningful celebration of our collective achievements and a reminder that, as one united&nbsp;<b>#JPFam</b>, we continue to grow, innovate, and embrace new opportunities together. ✨</p>

<p>We were deeply honoured to welcome&nbsp;<b>Mr Loo Cheng Chuan</b>, Founder of the <b>1M65 Movement</b>, Chairman of&nbsp;<b>Accss Digital</b>, and a valued&nbsp;<b>JPJC College Advisory Member</b>, as our Guest of Honour. In his inspiring address, titled&nbsp;<b>"Find Your Passion, Change the World,"</b>&nbsp;he encouraged our students to pursue what truly drives them, reminding us that passion, purpose, and perseverance are the foundations for creating meaningful impact in the world. 💬💖</p>

<p>Our principal,&nbsp;<b>Mr Ang,</b>&nbsp;opened his segment with a creative, partially AI-generated video featuring&nbsp;<b>Caleb</b>&nbsp;and&nbsp;<b>Kenji</b>, founders of the&nbsp;<b>JP Entrepreneurs Club</b>, who shared how JPJC nurtures creativity, innovation, and student agency across diverse disciplines, competitions, and initiatives. Their story reflected the enterprising spirit that continues to flourish within our college community. He then presented the College Report, celebrating JPJC's outstanding performance in the&nbsp;<b>2025 GCE A-Level Examinations</b>, alongside the many accomplishments of our students in leadership, service, sports, the arts, research, entrepreneurship, and beyond. He also highlighted how our vibrant learning culture, strong partnerships, and commitment to future-ready education continue to empower every student to thrive. 👏🌍

A big congratulations to&nbsp;<b>Emilie Sophie Bernadou Xin Xin</b>&nbsp;of&nbsp;<b>24S07</b>, our&nbsp;<b>2025 Valedictorian of the Year</b>. As Vice-President of the Debate Society, Emilie has exemplified intellectual curiosity, eloquence, compassionate leadership, and an unwavering commitment to excellence. Her remarkable journey reflects the values and aspirations of the JPJC community, inspiring others to pursue growth with courage and purpose. 🌟🚀📚

College Day is a celebration of the people, partnerships, and shared purpose that define the JPJC experience. As we honour our past and celebrate our present, we look ahead with confidence and optimism, ready to cross new frontiers and create even greater possibilities together as one&nbsp;<b>#JPFam**, with&nbsp;<b>#InfiniteLove</b>❤️,&nbsp;**#InfiniteGrowth</b>🌱, and&nbsp;<b>#InfinitePossibilities</b>🚀.
